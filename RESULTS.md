## 0. Reading guide

- **Paths** are relative to `ComplexSLE/`. A frozen theorem `Frozen/X.lean` has its provider in `Internal/X.lean`; a sealed classical input
  `External/X.lean` has its provider in `Internal/ExternalProofs/X.lean`.
- **Status is computed.** For the 36 sealed theorems (the 26 anchors of M9 and the ten sealed classical inputs of §5.1): `python3 -I tools/leanprobe.py externals-all frozen-anchor-manifest.json`
  (closure walk over type and value; `PROVED` = only `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`; `PROVED_REL{…}` = additionally
  `sorryAx`, all of it traced to EXTERNAL manifest exports, now only `hormander_oneDim` and `markov_oneDim`). For the internal declarations cited below (74 probed): the same closure
  walk (`leanprobe.externals_many`, imported from `tools/leanprobe.py` and batched; the CLI form `externals MODULE DECL` was re-run
  on `lemma_3_9` and `exists_lemma44_path` and agrees). Declarations named in Notes only as location pointers carry no status. Definitions carry no status;
  they are "definition (sealed)" (frozen, manifest SEALED) or "internal definition".
- **External short names** (full names in §5): only `hormander` (`hormander_oneDim`) and `markov_oneDim` are still EXTERNAL and
  appear in `PROVED_REL{…}`. The ten former externals (`ito_formula`, `timeChange_isContLocalMartingale`, `strongMarkov_sleDriver`,
  `blumenthal_sleDriver`, `support_sleDriver`, `birkhoff_stationary`, `recurrence_stationary`, `loewnerMap_conformal`,
  `loewnerMap_tendstoUniformly`, `exists_isBrownianReal`) are proved and sealed in place (R30), have status PROVED and are cited by
  their Lean names or E-numbers. Six others were deleted (§5.3).
- **hMO.** Theorems marked *[hMO]* take the hypothesis `hMO : MarkovOneDim` (the statement of the external `markov_oneDim`, R29).
  The probe cannot see that hypothesis, so their status omits `markov_oneDim`; the roots (and `converse_w_o_upgrade`, `lemma_3_9`)
  discharge it with `External.markov_oneDim` and then list it.
- **Coverage tag** (first word of Notes): `FULL` = statement formalized as in the source (typo fixes only);
  `RULED` = formalized with author-approved modifications (R-items named); `PARTIAL` = weaker, instance-only,
  driver-level or hypothesis-form (details given); `EXT` = content is an assumed EXTERNAL, no in-project proof;
  `NONE` = no Lean counterpart.
- **Numbering.** This file, the Lean docstrings, ERRATA.md and STATUS.md all use the paper numbering (§8.1 lists the earlier
  internal numbers, which survive only in the module file names `Lemma37*.lean`, `Lemma38*.lean`).
- **Adapted drivers.** Some internal theorems are stated for `IsSLEDriverAdapted` drivers (an `IsSLEDriver` with everywhere-continuous
  paths, adapted to a filtration with independent increments), a stronger hypothesis than the source's. Every `IsSLEDriver` has such
  a version almost surely (`IsSLEDriver.exists_adapted`), and the sealed roots take plain `IsSLEDriver`.

### 8.4 Third-party code and the Koebe constant

`ComplexSLE/Infrastructure/Zipper/` (9 files, each a `module` with a provenance header naming the upstream path) is ported from
leobon12/lean-formalizations @31e7483, at the author's direction (R30). That repository has no license file, and this one has none either; R30 records that
this is to be settled before any public release. Users: `Internal/ExternalProofs/ExistsIsBrownianReal.lean` (BMExistence, with GaussianSeries, CircleContinuity,

The sharp Koebe 1/4 theorem (it needs the area theorem) is not formalized. The proofs use the ported covering theorem
`QuantumZipper.CA.Koebe.ball_subset_image_koebe` (`B(f c, r‖f'(c)‖/48) ⊆ f(B(c, r))`), i.e. the universal constant `koebeK = 48`

constants are retuned (`koebeC` = log (koebeK²), `koebeN`, `lvlBase`, the bound 8·koebeK·δ'; rows 37, 39, 44) and no frozen statement changes. The
`1/4` theorem implies the `1/48` one.
