## Proof route (orchestrator blueprint)

Proof-route substitutions, all elementary and checked by hand:

1. **Planar topology without the Jordan curve theorem.** "By topological considerations" (L2194) and "hits every
   left-right crossing" (E_4) are replaced by two lemmas proved with a *chain argument*. For a ρ-chain β_0..β_n
   (consecutive distance < ρ), Φ(w) = Σ_k Arg((w-β_k)/(w-β_{k+1})) is continuous wherever every |w-β_k| > ρ. It
   satisfies exp(iΦ) = r/|r| with r = (w-β_0)/(w-β_n), and Φ → 0 at ∞.
   - **(P2) two connected sets.** Let A (connected, compact) lie in the strip |Im| < ε, and B (connected, compact) avoid
     the ball B_{3ε}(x0). Suppose both contain a point q with Re q ≤ x0-ε and a point p with Re p ≥ x0+ε. Then for every
     z₊ ∈ B_ε(x0+2iε) and z₋ ∈ B_ε(x0-2iε), A ∪ B disconnects z₊ or z₋ from ∞.
     Proof: if both lie in the unique unbounded component, a path between them gives Θ on A ∪ B. Θ - Arg r is constant
     on B. The IVT on A then hits a point where r > 0, which lies off the strip.
   - **(CL) chain crossing.** Let B (connected, compact) lie in |Re| < ε and reach above Im = h and below Im = -h. Let
     β be a ρ-chain with |Im β_k| ≤ h - 2ρ, Re β_0 ≤ -ε and Re β_n ≥ ε. Then some β_k is within ρ of B.
     Proof: Φ is continuous on B. Comparing it with Arg along the vertical rays from the two far points of B gives
     opposite signs. But Φ(B) cannot meet 2πℤ, because within |Re| < ε the line through β_0 and β_n lies inside the
     segment between them.
2. **Lemma 4.3, second bullet (Hurwitz) not needed.**
   - Upper semicontinuity of L_T is E12.
   - Lower semicontinuity (L_T has a point near ±2√T i) uses Koebe: dist(z, L_T) ≤ 4|f_T(z)|/|f_T'(z)| at
     z = i(2√T+ε'). Here f_T' → f_T^0' by the Cauchy estimates from E12.
   - Right hulls use the in-project duality `rightHull_dualDriver` / `leftHull_dualDriver` (Duality/Deterministic.lean),
     since the dual driver is uniformly small when U is.
3. **Lemma 4.6 (the c-event) replaced by a support-theorem event.** For c ≥ 0 a single deterministic path
   φ = s(t)(√a + i√b), with s piecewise linear, lies in range(Σ) and satisfies (cond-1..4) with margins. The event is
   {sup_{t≤2} |W_t - φ_t| < η} for the shifted driver W. Lemma 4.5 is then a pathwise ODE estimate
   (f_t = z + ∫ 2/f - V_t), and c < 0 follows from conjugation (`reflection_leftHull`, `IsSLEDriver.conjugate`).
4. **Thm 1.10(a) upgrade** is the M7-E5 pattern applied to the event Disc(u, z) = ∃ t > 0, z ∉ L_t ∧ L_t disconnects z.
   - Λ ≠ ∅ comes directly from Lemma 4.1, and Λ is open because disconnection is an open condition.
   - The R27 hitting lemma is used at a fixed rational σ-time, followed by the Koebe-free lower bound (Fatou plus
     compactness) and the Lévy 0-1 step.
   - Future dependence needs only the direction "the restarted hull disconnects f_σ(z) ⇒ L disconnects z" (the
     pullback lemma).

## Lemma 4.1 assembly (with R20)

Fix C (from 3), x = 2C, y = 7C, ε ≤ 1/3 and T large. Then set δ (R20) and K large.

| Event | Interval | Increment condition |
|---|---|---|
| E1 | [0,T] | sup\|U\| < η1 |
| E23 | [T,T+2] | sup\|W - φ\| < η2 |
| E4 | [T+2,T+2+K] | sup\|W'\| < η3 |

The three events are independent (`indep_shift_past`) and each has positive probability by E13. On their intersection:
- R_T is connected (`lemma_2_4_rightHull`), contains 0 (`zero_mem_rightHull`) and a point with Re ≥ y+3ε, and lies in
  |Im| < δ.
- Take ρ-chains in R_T, map them by F = f_2^{W}, and apply (CL) to L_K(W'). A compactness limit then gives a point
  β* ∈ R_T with F(β*) ∈ L_K(W').
- By `concatenation_leftHull`, β* ∈ L' = L_{2+K}(W), with Re β* ≥ x+2ε, and L' ∩ B_{3ε}(x) = ∅.
- (P2) with A = R_T and B = L' shows that one of f_T(U^±) is disconnected by R_T ∪ L'.
- The pullback lemma and `concatenation_leftHull` then show that L_{T+2+K} disconnects U^+ or U^-.
