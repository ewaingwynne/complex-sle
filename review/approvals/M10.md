## Where they are used (cut analysis, 2026-11-01)

Every use sits under four internal theorems, all about the concrete process θ̂ built from an SLE driver:
- **C1–C3:** one-time stationarity of θ̂ mod π under `P ⊗ ν`, where `ν` is the admissible density. C1–C3 are three
  forms of this one fact, in `Stationary/SLEStat.lean` and `Phases/ErgodicMean.lean`.
- **C4:** `law(θ̂_1(z)) ≪ Lebesgue` (`Phases/AllZDensity.lean`). It is used only in the Lemma 3.3 step from a.e. `z`
  to every `z`.

Re-proving C1–C4 with unchanged statements removes both externals; nothing downstream changes, except that the
`hMO` binders become dead.

What a re-route of C1–C3 needs, in place of hypoelliptic smoothing:
- the restart kernel `Q_r`, with Chapman–Kolmogorov from strong Markov (largely in-project already);
- Feller continuity of `Q_r`, from pathwise continuity of θ̂ in the starting angle;
- a martingale strong law, giving pathwise occupation measures whose limit points `μ` satisfy `∫ Lf dμ = 0`;
- uniqueness of probability measures on the circle with `∫ Lf dμ = 0`. In the case `c² < ab` this is an elementary
  distributional ODE argument. In the critical case `c² = ab` an atom at the zero of `β` must be ruled out.

From these, θ̂ is ergodic from every starting point, and Feller continuity makes `ν` invariant, which is C1–C3.

C4 needs a separate argument: a Lamperti transform plus a change of measure without stochastic integrals for
`c² < ab`. The critical case is again the hard part. Alternatively, the ergodic theorem from every starting point
could replace the Lemma 3.3 upgrade at its three call sites.
